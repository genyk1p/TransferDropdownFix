Transfer Dropdown Fix 0.1.2

Vessel names in the closed drop-downs of the Konstruction "Transfer resources" window.
Konstruction's own files are not modified. Not affiliated with Umbra Space Industries.
Requires Konstruction and Harmony 2.2.1+. Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
